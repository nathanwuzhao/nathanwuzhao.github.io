Challenge

Caregiver shortages leave many older adults without help for everyday tasks like fetching objects. The goal was a low-cost mobile manipulator that can pick something up from a table or counter and bring it to the user. That means a base small enough for a home (under 350 × 350 mm) that can still reach over 800 mm high and 600 mm out. The arms need enough dexterity to grip everyday objects, and the robot has to be simple to teleoperate, with vision assisting the grasp.

4th Grand Award, Robotics & Intelligent Machines, Regeneron ISEF 2025. Grand Winner, GKCSEF 2025.

System

Solid lines carry signals; dashed lines are battery power to the BTS7960s. The 5 V buck powers the microcontrollers and logic. Each LGT8F328P closes its own encoder loop locally, so only setpoints and telemetry cross the I²C bus.

Control

LaptopRuns the teleop interface and the vision pipeline on the overhead camera stream. Sends joint and drive commands to the robot over Wi-Fi.
ESP32Receives commands over Wi-Fi and dispatches setpoints over I²C to the subsystem controllers and the PCA9685.
LGT8F328P ×3One per subsystem (drive base, lift, extension). Runs PIDF on quadrature encoder feedback, with trapezoidal motion profiles for smooth moves and a feedforward term that holds position under load.
PCA9685Generates PWM for the shoulder and wrist servos.

The drive base navigates by dead reckoning from wheel encoders. For assisted grasping, the laptop runs camera frames through OpenCV and YOLOv5, tracks the detected object's center, and uses feedforward to align the servos with it. When the end effector's laser ToF reports the object in range, the gripper closes automatically.

Mechanisms

Drive baseTwo-wheel differential drive
Lift (×2)Independent per arm. Worm-gear, belt-driven vertical lift, with the arm carriage on a V-wheel Z-axis plate.
ShoulderServo pivot
ArmTelescoping tube, extended by a worm-gear-driven winch
Wrist2-DOF differential
End effectorCompliant-wheel mechanical gripper with a polycarbonate roller plate and a laser ToF distance sensor
